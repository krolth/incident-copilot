Capstone · RAG & Agents

Does a team of AI agents triage an outage better than one agent?

We built 18 incidents on a fictional chess platform, each with a known root cause, and had three agent designs triage every one of them, three times, on two models.

Open the incident console See all results

The experiment

Each run gets one alert and four tools: log search, metrics, deploy history and a knowledge base of runbooks and public postmortems (RAG). It must name the root-cause category, the failing component and the mitigation, or say there is not enough evidence. Three incidents have no single answer, so abstaining is the right call there. How it works

Finding 1

With a capable model, one agent beats a team

Root-cause category correct, on the 15 incidents that were not used to tune the prompts (45 runs per bar). On the small local model the three designs tie; on the hosted model they separate.

Finding 2

The team costs more and gets less right

Held-out accuracy against cost per run. Hollow points are the local model, priced at what the same model costs from a hosting provider.

Finding 3

They fail by giving up

How all 54 runs of each configuration ended. "Abstained wrongly" means it said "insufficient evidence" on an incident that had a clear cause.

Limits

18 incidents is small.

Six per difficulty tier. Repeats of one incident are not independent, so the site also compares designs incident by incident.

Retrieval is an upper bound.

The same person wrote the incidents and the runbooks the agents retrieve, so the runbooks fit the incidents better than real ones would.

Prompts were tuned on three incidents.

s01, s08 and s11. Headline numbers leave them out ("held-out"); dev accuracy is much higher.

The LLM judge is not validated yet.

Action and evidence scores come from a second model (qwen3.8:27b). They are marked pending until compared with an incident responder's hand labels. Category accuracy does not depend on the judge.

Two models, one setting.

One small open model and one hosted model, both at low reasoning effort, with prompts frozen before either full run.

Synthetic data.

The platform, incidents, logs and metrics are invented. No employer data.
