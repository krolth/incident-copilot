id: RB-02
title: Mitigate saturation of chess-db or Redis
category: dependency_saturation
---
## When to use
A shared store (chess-db, redis-live, redis-cache) is overloaded, its clients time out or queue, and no recent change
to those clients explains the load. Mitigate first: take load off the store, then find out why it rose.

## Signals
- chess-db: CPU near 100%, `connections` near the PgBouncer pool (300) or a client's cap, slow-query log dominated
  by one statement, "remaining connection slots are reserved" or PgBouncer "no more connections allowed".
- A query that was fast for months can turn slow without any deploy: as a table grows, outdated statistics or a
  switch to a generic plan for a prepared statement can make the planner pick a sequential scan. The slow-query log
  shows the same statement with a much higher duration and rows scanned.
- A scheduled job (`ratings-recalc`, `anticheat-backfill`) in the slow-query log or `pg_stat_activity` at onset.
- Redis: `hit_ratio` falls to near zero after a restart or flush (cold cache) and reads fall through to chess-db;
  or `used_memory_pct` near its limit with evictions rising.
- Client traffic (gateway `request_rate_rps`) is normal. If clients' traffic jumped, see RB-04; if a client's
  request rate to the store jumped right after its deploy or config push, see RB-01.

## Steps
1. Find the heaviest consumer: top statements in the slow-query log and `pg_stat_activity`, connections per client,
   running scheduled jobs; for Redis, the client sending the most commands.
2. Stop or throttle it:
   - a scheduled or ad hoc job: pause it (`anticheat-backfill` can always wait);
   - a runaway query: cancel it with `pg_cancel_backend`, then `pg_terminate_backend` if needed;
   - a plan regression: run `ANALYZE` on the table, and if the bad plan comes from a prepared statement, have
     Data on-call set `plan_cache_mode = force_custom_plan` for that client; confirm with `EXPLAIN` that the index
     is used again.
3. Cold redis-cache: do not restart clients. Let the cache refill, rate-limit the heaviest read path (leaderboards
   first) at the gateway, and start `leaderboard-rebuild` early if leaderboards are empty.
4. Confirm the store's CPU and connections return to baseline, then resume paused work slowly.

## Do not
- Add client pods or raise pool sizes: more connections deepen the overload.
- Fail over chess-db to the replica to "get a fresh database": the same load follows, and failover needs Data on-call.
