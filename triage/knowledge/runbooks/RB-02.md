title: Mitigate dependency saturation
category: dependency_saturation
---
## When to use
A shared dependency (database, cache, session store, queue) is overloaded and its clients are timing out, and no recent change to the clients explains the load.

## Signals
- Database CPU, connection count, or slow-query logs spike while client traffic is normal.
- Connection pool exhausted messages across several services that share one database.
- Cache hit ratio drops to near zero after a flush or restart, and the backing database takes the full read load (cold cache).
- A scheduled or ad hoc batch job, report, or analytics query appears in the slow-query log at the time the incident starts.

## Steps
1. Find the heaviest consumer of the dependency (top queries, top clients, scheduled jobs).
2. Stop or throttle that consumer (pause the batch job, kill the long query, rate-limit the client).
3. For a cold cache, warm it gradually or limit request rate until the hit ratio recovers; do not restart clients.
4. Confirm the dependency's CPU and connection count return to baseline, then resume work slowly.

## Do not
Add more client instances; they open more connections and deepen the overload.
