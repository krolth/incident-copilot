id: RB-04
title: Respond to a traffic spike beyond capacity
category: traffic_spike
---
## When to use
Request rate is far above normal because of legitimate demand (an event, a launch, a promotion) and the service is running out of CPU or capacity.

## Signals
- Request rate rises several times above its usual daily pattern with no corresponding fault in logs.
- CPU is high across all instances evenly, not on one host.
- Autoscaling is at its configured maximum, or scaling events are failing or rate-limited.
- No relevant deploy; rate-limit (429) responses may be present because the system is protecting itself.

## Steps
1. Confirm the demand source (event calendar, marketing schedule, traffic by client).
2. Raise the autoscaling maximum and add instances manually if scaling is capped.
3. Shed low-priority traffic if needed (rate-limit anonymous or batch clients).
4. Watch CPU and latency settle; plan a capacity review after the event.

## Do not
Roll back deploys that happened earlier in the day without evidence they are related.
