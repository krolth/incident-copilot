id: RB-07
title: Handle a DNS, network, or external provider failure
category: infra_failure
---
## When to use
Calls to an external provider (identity provider, email provider) or through shared network services (internal
resolvers `dns-01`/`dns-02`) fail, while our own services and hosts look healthy. Mitigate by routing around the
failure; the provider or network team fixes the cause.

## Signals
- "could not resolve host", "no such host", SERVFAIL, or timeouts to a resolver; connection resets or TLS timeouts
  to a provider.
- Failures limited to the paths that use that name or provider: for example social login fails while password login
  works; verification emails stop while everything else is fine.
- Our services' CPU and memory are normal; errors are on outbound calls.
- Other anomalies can be present at the same time (a scheduled job, an unrelated deploy). Decide which one lines up
  with the first failures before acting on it.

## Steps
1. From an affected pod, test resolution and connectivity (`dig <name> @dns-01`, `dig <name> @dns-02`,
   `dig <name> @<public resolver>`, `curl -v`) to separate a resolver problem from a network or provider problem.
2. Resolver problem: take the failing resolver out of rotation so pods use the healthy one; page Edge on-call.
3. Provider problem: check the provider's status page, contact them with timestamps and error samples, and degrade
   gracefully: show "social login unavailable, use email and password" and queue emails for retry.
4. Keep the workaround until the primary path has been healthy for thirty minutes.

## Do not
- Treat expected scheduled work (`ratings-recalc`, `db-backup`) as the cause without checking that its timing lines up.
- Restart the accounts service: it will fail the same way.
