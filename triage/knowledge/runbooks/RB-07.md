id: RB-07
title: Handle a network, DNS, or upstream provider failure
category: infra_failure
---
## When to use
Calls to an external provider or a shared network service fail, while internal services and hosts look healthy.

## Signals
- Name resolution failures ("could not resolve host", SERVFAIL, timeouts to a resolver) or connection resets to a provider.
- Failures are limited to the paths that call the affected provider or resolver.
- Several unrelated anomalies may be present at once (for example a scheduled batch job); decide which one lines up with the first failures.
- Internal metrics (CPU, memory) of the failing service are normal.

## Steps
1. Test resolution and connectivity from an affected host to separate DNS, network, and provider problems.
2. Fail over to the secondary resolver or provider route if one exists.
3. Contact the provider or network team with timestamps and error samples.
4. Keep the failover in place until the primary has been healthy for thirty minutes.

## Do not
Treat expected scheduled work (nightly batch jobs, backups) as the cause without checking timing.
