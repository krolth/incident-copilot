id: RB-03
title: Contain a resource leak
category: resource_leak
---
## When to use
A resource (memory, file descriptors, disk, connections) grows steadily over hours or days and approaches its limit, and no recent change explains the growth.

## Signals
- A slow, steady upward trend, often invisible in a one-hour view. Look at six hours, 24 hours, or seven days.
- Log messages such as "too many open files", out-of-memory kills, or "no space left on device" at the end of the trend.
- No deploy or configuration change near the start of the trend.
- Usage that does not fall when traffic falls.

## Steps
1. Confirm the trend over a long window and estimate time until the limit is reached.
2. Capture diagnostics first (heap dump, open descriptor list, disk usage by directory).
3. Restart the affected instances one at a time to reclaim the resource and watch that the trend resets.
4. File a defect with the diagnostics and the growth rate; schedule a fix.

## Do not
Restart every instance at once, or restart before capturing diagnostics.
