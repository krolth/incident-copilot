id: RB-08
title: Escalate with insufficient evidence
category: insufficient_evidence
---
## When to use
The available data cannot support a single conclusion: logs are missing, signals conflict, or the alert has already cleared.

## Signals
- A gap in log coverage during the incident window, or a metric with no data.
- The anomaly resolved before triage, leaving only a few unexplained errors.
- Two or more explanations remain equally plausible after checking logs, metrics, and deploys.

## Steps
1. State plainly that the cause is not established; do not guess a root cause.
2. List what was checked, what was missing, and which explanations remain.
3. Page the service engineer with those findings, or set a watch if the alert has cleared.
4. Record the missing data source as a follow-up.

## Do not
Pick the most recent deploy as the cause just because it is the only visible change.
